# Windows audio and video orientation fixes

An optional system or microphone track can end with no usable samples when Recall opens and pauses recording. The WASAPI callback previously marked a track as received even when `BytesRecorded` was zero. That zero-length WAV was passed to Media Foundation's AAC sink and raised `0xC00D4A44` (`MF_E_SINK_NO_SAMPLES_PROCESSED`). `AudioTrackCapture` now counts written sample bytes. It omits a track only when it contains at most two complete AAC frames; errors from stopping or encoding a track with sufficient samples still reach the existing error handler.

The cutoff comes from an isolated local test of NAudio's actual `MediaFoundationEncoder` with generated 48 kHz, stereo, 32-bit float audio. Segments of 1, 10, 20, 40, 41 and 42 ms produced `0xC00D4A44`; 43, 44, 45, 50, 55, 60, 80, 100 and 250 ms encoded successfully. An exact-sample probe narrowed the boundary: 2048 samples failed, while 2049 samples encoded and decoded to PCM. The policy therefore requires **more than** two 1024-sample AAC frames. The test generated its own samples and did not open a screen or microphone capture device.

The playback rotation issue is distinct from container metadata. Five recently completed local recordings had identity MP4 track matrices and 720 × 450 encoded and track dimensions, matching the selected 16:10 display after scaling. For each, a frame near a corresponding still was compared in memory under 0°, 90°, 180° and 270° candidates. All five matched 270° best with correlation scores of 0.993–0.999; for example, one set of scores was `[0.283, -0.013, 0.286, 0.993]`. This shows that the encoded pixels are turned 90° clockwise while the MP4 declares no rotation. Only container dimensions, matrices and numerical comparison scores were reported; no frame image or user filename was exported.

At playback, Recall compares the selected still with a decoded frame after the seek completes. The frame is reduced on the GPU to at most 160 pixels on its longest edge before any pixel readback. The comparison is attempted for at most five nearby frames and applies a rotation only when normalized luminance and edge correlation score at least 0.62 and exceed the next candidate by at least 0.10. Weak or unavailable matches retain the existing metadata correction and manual rotation control. A user pressing Rotate during the comparison cancels automatic correction for that playback.

The recorded pixels are also squeezed into the landscape MP4 frame. After a confident match, the output canvas is fitted to the still's aspect ratio as well as rotated; a subsequent manual quarter-turn swaps the fitted aspect. Diagnostics expose only angles, scores, attempt counts, dimensions and fitted bounds. Pure orientation and audio policy tests plus Media Foundation short-clip encoding tests pass in the core suite (152 checks). Native playback of the synthetic orientation fixtures and real user playback remain separate visual acceptance checks.
